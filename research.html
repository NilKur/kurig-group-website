Research

Electrochemical methods for sustainable value creation

We develop electrochemical methods that create value from renewable resources under real application conditions. Our focus is on complex, real-world feeds rather than idealized model systems. Turning them into chemicals, energy carriers and clean product streams depends on robust electrodes, well-designed cells and smart ways of applying current.

{% include wave.svg %}
01

Biogenic value chains

We start from what renewable value chains actually supply: biogenic residues, waste streams and bio-based intermediates. Instead of purified model compounds, we work with complex, variable feeds and turn them into products with real value.

02

Electrode materials

Complex feeds put electrodes under stress. We develop and characterize electrode materials, from robust carbon electrodes to materials tailored to specific feeds and processes. We study how they perform and age in authentic mixtures.

03

Cells for continuous operation

We design electrochemical cells tailored to complex feed streams. We operate them continuously and dynamically under technically relevant conditions, from lab scale toward application.

Across all topics

Alternating current electrolysis

We use alternating and pulsed current as a methodological tool that connects all three topics. Switching polarity can steer selectivity and regenerate electrode surfaces during operation.

For the selective conversion of complex biogenic mixtures, alternating polarity is a largely unexplored field.

ElectroBioUp

Upgrading complex renewable feedstock in an electrified biorefinery under application conditions. The group is built around this project.

  • Design of electrodes and electrolytes for efficient processes
