Research

Electrochemical methods for sustainable value creation

We develop electrochemical methods that create value from renewable resources, aiming at real application conditions. Our focus is on complex, real-world feeds rather than idealized model systems. Turning them into chemicals, energy carriers and clean product streams depends on robust electrodes, well-designed cells and smart ways of applying current.

01

Biogenic value chains

We start from what renewable value chains actually supply: biogenic residues, waste streams and bio-based intermediates. Instead of purified model compounds, we work with complex, variable feeds and turn them into valuable chemicals and fuels.

02

Electrode materials

Complex feeds put electrodes under stress. We develop and characterize electrode materials, from robust electrodes for synthesis to tailored materials for sustainable applications. We study how they perform and age in real-world environments.

03

Cells for continuous operation

We design electrochemical cells for synthesis and sustainable applications, tailored to complex feed streams. We operate them continuously and dynamically under technically relevant conditions, from lab scale toward technical scale.

Across all topics

Alternating current electrolysis

We use alternating and pulsed current as a methodological tool that connects all three topics. Switching polarity can steer selectivity, regenerate electrode surfaces during operation and enable dynamic, switchable processes.

For the selective conversion of complex biogenic mixtures, alternating polarity is a largely unexplored field.
