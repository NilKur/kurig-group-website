Dr. Nils Kurig

Independent Group Leader (CZS Nexus program)

Nils leads an independent junior research group at the Institute of Technical Chemistry, University of Stuttgart, funded through the CZS Nexus program of the Carl Zeiss Stiftung. He did his doctorate in technical chemistry at RWTH Aachen University, with a focus on electrochemical reaction engineering and biomass valorization. As a Leopoldina postdoctoral fellow with Phil Baran at Scripps Research, he added synthetic organic electrochemistry. His group brings both together to develop sustainable electrochemical methods under real application conditions.
